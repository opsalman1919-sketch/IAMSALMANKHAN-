Our 9 Expert Tips For False Ceiling

Tip 1: Check Room Height

Ensure room height is at least 9.5 feet before installing ceiling, otherwise room will look small.

Tip 2: Lighting Selection

Use combination of warm light and COB lights. Dim lights are best for TV Lounge.

Tip 3: Gypsum vs POP

Gypsum is stronger and durable, POP is cheaper. Gypsum is best for long term use.

Tip 4: Islamic Designs

Tip 5: Waterproofing

Tip 6: Budget Planning

Tip 7: Wooden Touch

Tip 8: 3D Designs
